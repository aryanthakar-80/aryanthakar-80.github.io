01 - About Me

About Me

I'm an Electronics & Instrumentation Engineering student at Nirma University with a passion for building things that work in the real world, from bare-metal firmware on STM32 microcontrollers to AI-vision expansion boards deployed in STEM robotics kits.

I thrive at the intersection of embedded systems, robotics, and control theory, writing low-level drivers one day and tuning sensor fusion algorithms the next. If it has a microcontroller, a motor, or a sensor, I'm interested.

Strengths

  • Digs deep into datasheets until things work.
  • Comfortable from bare-metal C to high-level Python.
  • Iterates fast through prototype, test, and ship cycles.

Current Edges

  • Scope creep: every project wants one more feature.
  • Learning to budget more time for PCB spins.
  • Still tempted to debug hardware at 2 AM.
